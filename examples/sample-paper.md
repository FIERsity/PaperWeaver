# A Small Study of Transparent Research Workflows

## Abstract

This fictional paper examines whether explicit provenance notes help readers inspect a research workflow.

## Introduction

Reproducibility depends on both methods and understandable records.

## Methods

We compare two short documentation templates in a simulated review exercise.

## Results

Readers found the provenance-first template easier to inspect.

## Discussion

The result is illustrative only and does not establish effects in real publishing settings.
